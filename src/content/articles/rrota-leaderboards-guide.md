# Complete Guide to RROTA Weekly, Monthly, Yearly and All-Time Leaderboards

RROTA Spin-to-Win is built around more than a single game session or one short race.

The platform connects four leaderboard periods:

- **Weekly leaderboard**
- **Monthly leaderboard**
- **Yearly leaderboard**
- **All-time leaderboard**

Each board gives players a different way to measure participation.

The weekly leaderboard creates a short competition window. The monthly board rewards consistency across a longer period. The yearly board creates a championship path. The all-time board preserves a broader history of player activity.

Together, these rankings turn spins, missions, bonuses, referrals, progression, and other eligible actions into an ongoing competition system.

> **Important:** Current prizes, deadlines, qualifying positions, scoring rules, boosts, and eligibility conditions can change. Always check the live Spin-to-Win interface and the latest official RROTA announcement before relying on campaign information.

## Why RROTA Uses Four Leaderboard Periods

A single leaderboard favors only one type of participant.

A short race rewards immediate activity. A longer race rewards consistency. A historical board recognizes participation over time.

RROTA uses several ranking periods to create both urgency and continuity.

### Weekly competition creates fresh opportunities

A weekly reset gives players a new race window.

This helps create:

- Regular deadlines
- New opportunities to compete
- Frequent winner announcements
- Repeat community activity
- A clear beginning and end for each short race

A player who joins later does not always need to compete against months of historical activity.

### Monthly competition rewards consistency

The monthly leaderboard follows a longer period.

It can recognize players who remain active across more than one weekly race rather than only during one unusually strong day.

### Yearly competition creates a championship path

The yearly leaderboard connects many shorter periods into one long-term competition.

It gives active players a reason to continue participating beyond a single weekly or monthly race.

### All-time ranking preserves history

The all-time leaderboard provides a broader record of account activity.

It can recognize long-term participants even after shorter competition periods reset.

An all-time position does not automatically mean that an all-time reward exists. A reward applies only when the live product or an official campaign clearly states it.

## The Weekly RROTA Leaderboard

The weekly leaderboard is the shortest recurring competition layer.

It creates a fresh race window on a regular schedule.

### What the weekly board may display

Depending on the current interface, it may show:

- Current player position
- Eligible score or activity
- Remaining race time
- Nearby competitors
- Current qualifying positions
- Player identifier
- Campaign-specific information

### Why the weekly board resets

A weekly reset creates a new competition rather than allowing one historical score to dominate every future race.

This supports:

- New entry opportunities
- More frequent race deadlines
- Repeat participation
- Regular campaign updates
- More frequent winner-review cycles

### What happens when a weekly race ends

When the timer reaches zero, the displayed ranking may become subject to review.

The review can include:

- Account validity
- Qualifying activity
- Duplicate-account checks
- Bot or automation checks
- Referral-quality review
- Exploit review
- Wallet information
- Campaign-specific requirements
- Other fair-play evidence

The leaderboard may reset for the next race while the completed race is still under review.

A reset does not remove the need to verify the previous result.

## Leaderboard FAQ

### Do weekly scores carry into the next weekly race?

A weekly leaderboard normally follows its own competition period and reset schedule. The next weekly race begins with a new short-period ranking.

Longer leaderboard periods may still preserve qualifying activity according to their own rules.

### Can a player compete in weekly and monthly rankings at the same time?

Yes. The platform can run several ranking periods at once. The same eligible activity may contribute to more than one active board depending on the current scoring system.

### Does the monthly board reset every week?

No. The monthly leaderboard follows its own period rather than the weekly reset.

### Does the yearly board reset monthly?

No. The yearly leaderboard follows the active yearly competition schedule.

### Does the all-time leaderboard reset?

The all-time board is intended as a broader historical ranking. Current product behavior should still be checked in the live interface.

### Does first place automatically receive a prize?

No. A displayed position can remain subject to campaign rules, account requirements, qualifying activity, wallet information, fair-play checks, and final eligibility review.

### Can a player be disqualified after the timer ends?

Yes. Review can identify bots, duplicate accounts, fake referrals, exploits, ineligible activity, or other rule violations after the live race closes.

### Are old reward posts still valid?

Not necessarily. Always check the active campaign and current official announcement.

### Is holding $RTA required to play?

General product access and holder-linked benefits are separate questions. Current access requirements and campaign boosts should be checked in the live platform.

### Can RROTA support recover a wallet?

No. RROTA cannot recover a seed phrase, private key, wallet password, or recovery code.
