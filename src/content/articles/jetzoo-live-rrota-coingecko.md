RROTA has two new milestones: **JETZOO is publicly playable**, and **RROTA ($RTA) is listed on CoinGecko**.

## Play JETZOO

[JETZOO](https://jetzoo.fun) is a standalone browser arcade runner in the RROTA ecosystem. It adds another playable experience alongside [Spin-to-Win](https://spin.rrota.xyz).

JETZOO's current release focuses on arcade gameplay. RTA payments, token rewards, and shared progression with Spin-to-Win are not part of this release.
